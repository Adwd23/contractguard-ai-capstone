# Corporate Procurement Policy

## Payment Terms
Standard payment terms are Net 30 days after receipt of a correct invoice and acceptance of the relevant deliverables. Shorter payment terms require a documented exception approved by Procurement.

## Approval Thresholds
A contract with a total value of SAR 500,000 or more requires explicit approval by both Procurement and Legal before signature. The approval record must be retained with the contract audit trail.

## Invoice Controls
Invoices must reference a valid purchase order, itemize accepted services, and be subject to dispute and withholding rights for incomplete or non-conforming deliverables.
