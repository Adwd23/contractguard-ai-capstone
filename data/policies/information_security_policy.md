# Information Security Minimum Standard

## Baseline Controls
All critical vendors must maintain ISO 27001 certification or demonstrably equivalent controls. Sensitive data must be encrypted in transit with modern TLS and encrypted at rest using managed keys.

## Access Control
Access must follow least privilege, multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, quarterly access reviews, and prompt revocation. Administrative actions must produce tamper-evident audit logs.

## Assurance and Testing
Critical vendors must complete annual penetration testing, remediate critical vulnerabilities within 15 days, provide independent assurance reports, and permit reasonable customer audit rights.

## Incident Management
Security incidents must be contained, investigated, documented, and reported according to the 24-hour notification rule in the Corporate Data Protection Policy.
