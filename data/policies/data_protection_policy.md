# Corporate Data Protection Policy

## Saudi Data Residency
Personal data and regulated business data must be stored and primarily processed inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. A cross-border transfer requires a documented legal mechanism, Data Protection Officer review, and prior written approval from the organization.

## Subprocessors
A vendor must provide advance notice of every subprocessor, obtain written consent where required, maintain an up-to-date subprocessor register, and flow down equivalent confidentiality, privacy, and security obligations. The organization must have an objection and termination right.

## Breach Notification
The vendor must notify the organization within 24 hours after discovering a suspected or confirmed personal-data or security breach. The notice must include scope, affected records, containment actions, and a remediation timetable.

## Data Subject and Deletion Duties
The vendor must support access, correction, deletion, retention, and return requests. Data must be securely deleted or returned at contract termination and deletion must be certified.
