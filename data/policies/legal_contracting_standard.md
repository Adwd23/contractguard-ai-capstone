# Legal Contracting Standard

## Liability and Indemnity
The vendor liability cap should be no lower than twelve months of fees. Confidentiality, intellectual-property infringement, privacy, security breach, fraud, and willful misconduct must have appropriate carve-outs or higher caps.

## Termination
The organization must have a termination-for-convenience right on no more than 30 days' notice. Automatic renewal periods should not exceed one year and must include a practical non-renewal window.

## Governing Law
Unless the General Counsel approves an exception, vendor contracts must be governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and disputes must be heard in competent Saudi courts.

## Mandatory Records
Material exceptions, approvals, redlines, and final signed terms must be captured in an immutable audit record.
