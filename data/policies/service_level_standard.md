# Enterprise Service Level Standard

## Availability
Critical hosted services must provide at least 99.9 percent monthly uptime, measured continuously and excluding only approved maintenance windows.

## Service Credits
The contract must define service credits for missed availability, chronic-failure termination rights, and transparent incident reporting.

## Support Response
Priority-one incidents require acknowledgement within 15 minutes, continuous remediation, executive escalation, and a written root-cause report within five business days.
